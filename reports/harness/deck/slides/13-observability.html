Observability Is a Harness Responsibility

  • Harnesses capture lifecycle, data flow, and full error context automatically.
  • Process artifacts explain why a change is accepted, not just what ran.
  • Evidence‑backed feedback prevents blind retries and wasted diagnosis.
Without observability, evaluation becomes subjective and handoffs reset progress.
